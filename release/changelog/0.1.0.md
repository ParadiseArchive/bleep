The first Bleep: little robots on tracks with a display for a face. For Minecraft 26.3 and 26.1.2 with NeoForge.

- **Build your own.** Find a Bleep Core in a dungeon or an abandoned mineshaft (or craft one, at a price), and craft a Display, Tracks and Arms. Use the core on the ground with the other three and your bleep is put together part by part, until its screen comes on for the first time.
- **Eleven faces** on a display that glows in the dark: happy, excited, sad, angry, bored, surprised, tired, asleep, stubborn, glitching and the start-up screen. It blinks now and then, and its arms and head move with how it feels.
- **A voice.** A blip for every mood, a little higher or lower for every bleep.
- **A battery instead of health.** Driving and damage drain it; an empty bleep switches off with a sleepy face instead of dying.
- **A homebase.** A charging pad your bleep goes home to when it runs empty. Go Home sends it there.
- **More ways to charge.** Redstone, a power bank you carry along, and lightning, which overcharges it.
- **An info screen.** Right-click your bleep: its mood, what it is doing and thinking, its battery, your bond, its favourite item, and Follow, Stay and Go Home.
- **A bond you build.** Pet it, give it treats and its favourite item, spend days together. A new bleep sometimes dawdles over what you ask; a best friend hardly ever does.
- **It learns as the bond grows.** A Friend carries a torch that lights the way; a Buddy fights at your side and brings you what it finds.
- **Dress it up.** Dye it any colour; a party hat, a top hat or an antenna with a bow; star, heart and lightning stickers. Tricks on its display: its name as a ticker, tic-tac-toe, an arrow home.
- **Character.** A favourite item it is crazy about (hold it and it drives circles round you), a dry remark about every mob in the game, naughty tricks when it is bored (a stolen cookie, a moved torch), and a mysterious fear of llamas.
- **The Bleepedia.** A book on its info screen with every mob it met (with its face), the biomes it drove through, the special blocks it found, its stats and its diary. A ! when there is news.
- **Out in the world.** It dances to records, sleeps next to your bed, digs things up and adores iron golems. Find an abandoned bleep and wake it with a block of redstone.
- **Jade and JEI**, when you have them: battery, mood and bond in Jade, every recipe and where the core is found in JEI.
