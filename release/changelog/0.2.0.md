Photos, boats and a homebase with its own battery. For Minecraft 26.3 and 26.1.2 with NeoForge.

- **Photos.** At a nice moment your bleep says Click! and takes a photo of what you see: a sunset with you, a falling star, its best friend, a biome it has never been in. Twelve at most, in a new Photos tab in its Bleepedia; click one to see it big, and Print puts it on an empty map for an item frame.
- **A homebase battery.** A homebase now charges from a battery of its own, four bars on its post. Fill it with redstone (10%), a block of redstone (90%) or FE from any mod's cables. A comparator reads it, and a broken homebase keeps its charge. Homebases from 0.1.0 start empty: give them some redstone.
- **Hoppers and pipes** reach the storage of the bleep parked on a homebase.
- **A boat and a sled.** In the water a little boat pops up under it; on snow it rides a sled, half as fast again.
- **A snowman.** Told to stay somewhere snowy with nothing to do, it builds one, once a day. A carved pumpkin from its storage brings it to life.
- **A museum.** New Display Case: place a few near its homebase and it shows off its favourite item and best finds behind glass. You can fill them too.
- **A weather forecast.** It feels rain and thunder coming a minute early, with a cloud on its screen.
- **Guess who.** A bored bleep does an impression of a mob it met, and tells you who it was.
- **A whistle.** Blow it and your bleeps within 64 blocks come to you; sneak and blow, and they stay.
- **A Bleep Tracker.** All your bleeps on one screen: battery, what they do, and an arrow to each.
- **A mute button** on its info screen. On its homebase it is quiet anyway.
- **Thirteen advancements**, in a Bleep tab of their own.
- **A config for pack makers:** bleeps per player, battery drain, magnet range, homebase power, cores in chests, falling stars, abandoned bleeps.
- **Fixed:** a bleep asleep by your bed now wakes up with you in the morning.
