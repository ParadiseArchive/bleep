Races, a band, expeditions and a trailer. For Minecraft 26.3 and 26.1.2 with NeoForge.

- **Races.** New Start Flag and Race Flags. Put race flags around a start flag (within 32 blocks) and right-click the start flag: your bleeps line up, 3, 2, 1, GO! They drive to the nearest flag, each time on to the nearest one they have not been to, and back to the start. Everyone near hears the times, and every start flag keeps its course record. Other players standing at the start race with their bleeps too; one bleep alone is a time trial.
- **Music.** Play a tune (in Play) and your bleep drives to a note block and plays one of four tunes. Bleeps nearby join on note blocks of their own, up to three: melody, harmony and bass. Now and then a bleep with nothing to do plays by itself.
- **Expeditions.** Go exploring (in Play) and your bleep is away for a Minecraft day: a forest, a desert, the mountains, the snow, the ocean, the jungle, a swamp or a cave. It comes back next to you with things it found (sometimes something special), two photos (one a selfie), a story in its diary and a new biome in its Bleepedia. It needs half a battery to go.
- **Crafting by watching.** A best friend watches you craft and remembers how. The new crafting button on its info screen shows what it learned, and it makes it again from its storage: one, or as many as it can. Empty buckets go back in its storage.
- **A trailer.** Give it a Trailer and it pulls a little cart behind it: its storage becomes as big as a large chest. Shears take it off again once the back half is empty.
- **The Nether.** Bleeps that follow you now come along through a portal. In the Nether it slowly gets hot, faster near lava: too hot and it smokes, slows down and drains its battery until it has cooled off. It drives to water, a water cauldron, ice or snow to cool down, or hand it a snowball, ice or a water bottle.
- **The End.** It goes very quiet there and looks at the stars, and its Bleepedia gets a new sign.
- **Abandoned bleeps are rare now**, and only in special places: an ancient city, a trail ruin, a zombie village or, very rarely, a mineshaft. Each place rolls once. A bleep you wake up has an old photo from long ago and tells you its story, bit by bit.
- **Falling stars never land on what you built.** A star only comes down where everything 10 blocks round is nature, never within 48 blocks of a player's bed or respawn point, and its crater only takes natural blocks.
- **Three new advancements:** Band Practice, Bon Voyage and And They're Off! Sixteen in all; Good as New is a challenge now.
